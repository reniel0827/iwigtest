The House

Built on craft.
Gated by design.

iWIG sources premium human hair from a vetted network of ethical suppliers across Asia, Europe, and South America. Every piece is hand-inspected, hand-tied, and quality-graded before it enters our wholesale catalog.

We serve salons, boutiques, and stylists who refuse to compromise on what they carry. Our trade is gated — every applicant holds a valid business license, and every approved retailer receives the same wholesale tier. No inflated minimums. No preferential treatment.

What we stand for

  • Quality without exception. Every SKU passes two rounds of quality assurance before fulfillment.
  • Transparent origin. Source documentation provided on request.
  • Trade integrity. We do not sell to end consumers.
